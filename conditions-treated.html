Conditions Treated

Pain

We specialize in treating back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain, carpal tunnel syndrome, knee pain, whiplash, trauma, arthritis, numbness, tingling, nerve pain, headache, migraines, sciatica.

Immunity

We treat seasonal and food allergies, hay fever, asthma, chronic fatigue syndrome, fibromyalgia, rheumatoid arthritis, shingles, sinus infections, thyroid conditions.

Pediatrics

We use a massage technique called tuina to activate the meridians of a child. These meridians can all be accessed by gently massaging the child’s hand. We use acupressure, tuina, herbal therapy and occasionally pediatric acupuncture needles to treat ailments such as: diarrhea, constipation, ear infections, common colds, flu, malnutrition, failure to thrive, colic, restless sleep and bed wetting.

Mental and Emotional Issues

Such as stress, depression, anxiety, insomnia, and panic attacks.

Gastro-Intestinal Ailments

Stress related digestive issues, IBS, acid reflux, gall stones, nausea, vomiting, gas/bloating, celiac’s disease, hepatitis.

Women’s Health

We treat infertility, menstrual disorders, low libido, menopausal discomforts, prenatal and post-partum issues and pms.

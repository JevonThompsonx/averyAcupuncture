Accessibility

Avery Acupuncture is committed to making this website usable by everyone — including our older patients and people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnification, or keyboard-only navigation.

This site is built and tested to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA — the standard referenced by the U.S. Department of Justice and California accessibility law — and it is re-checked whenever anything on it changes.

If any part of this site is hard for you to use, please tell us. Call 805.400.9723 and we will help you directly, fix the problem, and confirm the fix with you. We would much rather hear about a barrier than have you work around it.
